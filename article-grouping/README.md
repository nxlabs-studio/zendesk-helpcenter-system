# Article Grouping System

## Overview

This document outlines a reconstructed article grouping concept for Zendesk Guide section pages.

The goal is to improve long article-list usability by grouping articles into clearer, topic-based sections instead of displaying one flat list.

---

## Problem

Legacy Help Center section pages often rely on long lists of article links.

This can create:

- poor scanning behavior
- weak topic separation
- excessive cognitive load
- difficulty locating related articles
- inconsistent article discovery

---

## Solution

The reconstructed grouping system organizes articles into logical topic groups.

Instead of showing one continuous article list, articles can be grouped by labels, configuration rules, or section-specific logic.

```plaintext
Section Page
├── Account Opening
├── Verification
├── Account Management
└── Security
